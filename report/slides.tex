\begin{frame}{OpenVINS --- and the dataset problem}
\begin{columns}[T]
\begin{column}{0.44\textwidth}
One camera $+$ an IMU. It never recognises places it has already been, so
errors only accumulate.

\vspace{0.6em}
From the takeoff point it starts \textbf{14.7\,m} out and ends \textbf{700\,m}
out after 3.6\,km.

\vspace{0.6em}
The \emph{shape} is right, but the path comes out \textbf{too small} --- as if
the flight were shrunk. Stretching it back fixes most of the error.

\vspace{0.8em}
\textbf{Why so few usable datasets?}\\[0.3em]
{\small Camera tracking wants to fly \textbf{low}\\
{\scriptsize things move more between frames}\\[0.2em]
Map matching wants to fly \textbf{high}\\
{\scriptsize each photo covers more ground}}
\end{column}

\begin{frame}{Which predictions can we trust?}
\begin{columns}[T]
\begin{column}{0.4\textwidth}
Only \textbf{36\%} of matches are right --- and the network's own confidence is
\emph{higher when it is wrong}:

\vspace{0.5em}
\centering
\begin{tabular}{@{}lcc@{}}
\toprule
confidence & correct & wrong \\
\midrule
flight 1 & 0.9429 & \textbf{0.9493} \\
flight 2 & 0.9447 & \textbf{0.9598} \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}

\vspace{1em}
\raggedright
\textbf{So we ignore confidence entirely.}

\vspace{0.7em}
Instead: keep only matches that \textbf{agree with each other}, once you account
for how far the aircraft moved between them.

\vspace{0.5em}
{\small Wrong matches land in random places --- two agreeing is a $0.5\%$
coincidence. Right ones agree automatically.}
\end{column}

\begin{frame}{Causal fusion}
\centering
$\hat{\bm{p}}_k = s\,\bm{R}(\theta)\,\bm{p}^{\text{vio}}_k + \bm{t}$
\quad --- estimated online, \textbf{from past predictions only}

\vspace{1.2em}
\begin{columns}[T]
\begin{column}{0.47\textwidth}
\raggedright
\textbf{On each new prediction}
\begin{enumerate}
\item enters a buffer
\item \emph{trailing} window screened for consensus
\item survivors accumulate
\item refit $(s,\theta,\bm{t})$ to all accepted history
\end{enumerate}

\vspace{0.9em}
\textbf{Before handover:} dead-reckon on VIO from the known takeoff pose\\
{\small $\Rightarrow$ position output at \emph{every} instant}
\end{column}
\begin{column}{0.47\textwidth}
\raggedright
\textbf{Why estimate $s$ and $\theta$?}\\[0.3em]
{\small The system starts not knowing which way is north, and the VIO path comes
out too small --- both must be learned from the matches.}

\vspace{1.2em}
\textbf{An EKF lost: 422\,m vs 347\,m}\\[0.3em]
{\small Scale error grows \emph{linearly}, process noise as $\sqrt{\cdot}$.
The filter turns overconfident and gates out correct predictions --- most
certain when most wrong.}
\end{column}
\end{columns}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Limitations and next steps}
\begin{columns}[T]
\begin{column}{0.5\textwidth}
\textbf{Availability, not accuracy, is the limit}
\begin{itemize}
\item 100\,m tile needs $\sim$66\,m altitude\\
      {\small only \textbf{34\%} of this flight qualifies}
\item Below that: 1--5\% correct\\
      {\small adding them made fusion \emph{worse}: 347 $\rightarrow$ 527\,m}
\item Smaller tiles collapse the descriptors\\
      {\small 2.21\% of pairs $>0.99$ similar, vs 0.31\% at 100\,m}
\item Altitude and initial heading still from ground truth\\
      {\small deployment: altimeter $+$ magnetometer}
\end{itemize}
\end{column}
\begin{column}{0.48\textwidth}
\textbf{Next}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Query-side scale normalisation}\\
      {\small one database at the lowest operating altitude; rescale queries down to it}
\item \textbf{Retrain on map tiles the right size}\\
      {\small trained on tiles covering 1438\,m of ground, used on 100\,m ---
      a $14\times$ mismatch}
\item Find out why the VIO path comes out too small\\
      {\small also the source of run-to-run spread: 540 vs 464\,m from
      identical inputs}
\end{enumerate}
